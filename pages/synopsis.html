Synopsis

BOOKS BY Jeff Lindsay
CREATOR James Manos Jr.
SHOWRUNNER Clyde Phillips
ORIGINAL RUN 2006–2013
SEASONS 8
EPISODES 96

DEXTER is a crime drama series created for television by James Manos Jr., based on Jeff Lindsay’s novels. It follows Dexter Morgan, a forensic expert who secretly hunts criminals.

James Manos Jr. developed Dexter for television and wrote the pilot episode. He helped turn Jeff Lindsay’s book into a TV series with its own characters and story.

The series follows Dexter Morgan, who works as a blood-spatter analyst for the Miami police. At the same time, he secretly lives as a serial killer who targets people he believes deserve to die.

Dexter premiered on Showtime in 2006 and ran for eight seasons. Michael C. Hall played Dexter, while many different writers, directors, and producers worked on the series throughout its run.
